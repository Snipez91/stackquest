# Team guide

## Setting up your team (admins)

1. Create an account and choose **Start a new team**. You become the team admin.
   The team's time zone (taken from your browser) defines when the weekly
   leaderboard resets: Monday 00:00 in that zone. You can change it under
   **Team admin → Team settings**.
2. Go to **Team admin → Invite teammates**. Choose:
   - **Expires after:** 1 hour to 30 days (3 days by default).
   - **Uses:** 1 person, up to 5, or up to 25. A single-use link per person is safest.
   - **Role:** Member, or Admin (admins can invite, change roles, remove members and
     moderate chat).
3. Copy the link **now**. StackQuest stores only a hash of the link, so it can't be
   shown again. Send it privately (DM or email).
4. You can **revoke** an active link at any time. Expired, used-up and revoked links
   show a clear message and never grant access.

New people who open the link create an account (or sign in) and join immediately.
Someone who is already a member can open the link without using it up.

## What teammates can see

Visible to your team: completed missions, track progress, badges, level, a summary
mastery band, leaderboard XP, and chat messages.

Private to you: your answers, wrong attempts, hint use, review results and draft
code. You can also hide your name on leaderboards (Settings). You still count
toward team goals.

## A healthy weekly rhythm

- **Daily (5 min):** clear the review queue. Missed items come back tomorrow with a
  refresher.
- **Weekly:** each person completes one or two missions. Admins set one or two
  cooperative goals (for example "Pass 25 reviews as a team").
- **Discuss:** use each mission's thread to compare approaches. Wrap answers in
  `||spoilers||`.
- **Races** are opt-in and don't affect XP or mastery. Nobody should feel rushed.
